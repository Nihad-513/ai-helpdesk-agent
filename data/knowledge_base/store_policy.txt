Bean & Brew Store Policy

SHIPPING POLICY
Standard shipping and delivery takes 3-5 business days.
Customers asking when an order will arrive or how long delivery takes should refer to this standard delivery timeframe.

RETURN POLICY
Customers can return products within 14 days after delivery.
The return window is 14 days from the date the product is delivered.
Customers who want to return an item, send a product back, or request a return must contact the Bean & Brew support team.

PAYMENT SUPPORT
If a payment fails, is declined, or does not go through, customers should contact the Bean & Brew support team for assistance.
For payment-related problems, failed transactions, or payment issues, customers should contact the support team.
